Cafe Laurel

Logo · Bilingual wordmark

A monogram where the C wraps a laurel leaf and the L carries a rising arrow. A coffee bean anchors the base. The name appears in English and Arabic so the café reads clearly to every guest.

Idea
Leaf, bean and growth in one letterform
Language

Shaheen

Logo · Fragrance brand

Shaheen means falcon. The S is drawn as one flowing line that becomes the bird's neck, head and plume, with small flowers beside it to point to the perfumes. The look is calm and premium.

Idea
A letter that is also a falcon
Tagline
Artisan Fragrances

JS Photography

Logo · Photographer identity

A single line draws the camera body and forms the letters J and S. The lens becomes an aperture with an S inside it. The wordmark is set in a classic serif for a trusted, timeless feel.

Idea
Initials and camera drawn as one shape
Type
Classic serif wordmark
